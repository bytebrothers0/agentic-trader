# Advanced MSNR Video 7 Notes

## The A+ Setup: STFL Inside FTFL
Building on the continuation logic from Video 6, Video 7 emphasizes the interaction between the First Time Frame Level (FTFL) and the Second Time Frame Level (STFL).

### The Core Entry Rule
To have an A+ entry, you must find a valid key level on the STFL that is located **completely inside** the FTFL box drawn on the higher time frame.

1. **Mark FTFL:** Draw your FTFL box on the first time frame (e.g., Daily) using the 1-Lap OCL or a newly formed QM.
2. **Switch to STFL:** Go down to the second time frame (e.g., 4H).
3. **Locate Key Levels:** Look for a fresh **Support, Resistance, or QM** on the STFL.
4. **Validation:** This STFL key level *must* be inside the FTFL box. If it is outside the box, it is not an A+ setup, and you should reconsider or reduce risk.

### Handling Un-Fresh STFL Levels
- If the STFL key level inside your FTFL box is not fresh (has been tested before), it is no longer valid.
- The rule of "freshness" applies universally across all time frames. A level is only good for its *first* retest.

### Trading Psychology and Discipline
- The Alchemist strategy requires strict discipline. If the market does not come back to your marked STFL inside the FTFL, **do not force a trade**.
- Sometimes the market will miss your level and continue; let it go. Trying to catch trades outside of these strict A+ parameters leads to losses.
- Focus exclusively on the top priority levels: Support, Resistance, and QM. Ignore RBS and SBR for the main structural entries.
