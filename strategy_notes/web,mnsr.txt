Entry rules — the codable core
Seven trendline rules from the web, all checks we can write: connect ≥2 SNRs, body closes not wicks (done — our body pivots), never connect GAP SNR (needs a gap filter: skip anchors where |open[i] − close[i−1]| > 0.3 ATR), no close beyond point 3 (already our break rule), enter only at point 3 on a wick touch + rejection (already our touch rule), never at point 2. Touch validity: first touch must be a wick, never a body; MISS candles (bars that skip past without touching) strengthen the level; sweep at the level = strongest signal. Our touches counter needs these two upgrades: reject body-first-touches, count MISS bars as support.
Engulfing (4 web types — regular, GAP, hidden = invisible on HTF, appears on LTF refinement, and flipped): the zone is marked between the two closes (top = higher close, bottom = lower close), limit orders live there. This is finer than our current containment flag — worth adopting exactly.
Scoring the X (weights from the web's emphasis): SNR fresh = base; +TL cross (the X itself); +engulfing at point 3 (mandatory — without it, skip, no exceptions); +sweep present (best tier); +2-TF confirmation; +M15 structure (BOS/MSS agreement). QMX (all of the above on a QML) = max size. SL beyond the zone extreme + buffer (never on the line — "zones, not lines"); minimum 1:2 RR; level usable max twice (fresh→unfresh→dead, body-break flips it to fresh SBR/RBS the other way).
