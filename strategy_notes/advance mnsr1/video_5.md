## FTFL, STFL, and TTFL Concepts
The Alchemist strategy introduces three crucial time frame levels:
- **FTFL (First Time Frame Level/Line):** This is the time frame where the storyline is established. It answers the question: *Where is the market coming to?*
- **STFL (Second Time Frame Level/Line):** The time frame used for entries. It answers: *Where will the market buy or sell from?*
- **TTFL (Third Time Frame Level/Line):** Used for refinement, allowing for a smaller Stop Loss (SL) and bigger Take Profit (TP).

## Marking the FTFL
When a storyline is just formed (e.g., Daily Reject Daily + 4H Breakout), you must return to the **First Time Frame** (e.g., Daily) to mark the FTFL.

### Top Priority Key Levels for FTFL
On the first time frame, there are only two primary key levels we look for to draw our FTFL:
1. **Support / Resistance**
2. **QM (Quasimodo Level)**

These two are considered **A+ setups** and are the strongest levels. Other levels like RBS (Resistance Becomes Support) or SBR (Support Becomes Resistance) are less strong and should generally be ignored for the FTFL.

### The "Freshness" Rule
For a key level to be a valid FTFL, it **must be fresh** (unmitigated).
- If the market has already returned to a Support/Resistance or QM and tested it, that level is no longer fresh.
- We do not trade from un-fresh levels. If the primary levels are mitigated, we skip them or look for continuation setups.

### Risk Management
- If the market returns to a fresh A+ key level (Support/Resistance/QM) on the FTFL, you can trade with your normal risk.
- If you are unsure or trading a less optimal setup (like an un-fresh level or weaker key level), you must reduce your risk significantly (e.g., from $100 to $10 or $20).
