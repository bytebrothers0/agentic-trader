## Core Mechanical Rules (IF/THEN Logic)

### 1. Protected Highs/Lows
*   **IF** a swing point (HL/LH) successfully caused a BOS:
    *   **THEN** This point is **PROTECTED**. Institutions have a vested interest in keeping price away from this level.
    *   **IF** price breaks this point, it is a **CHoCH** (Trend change).

### 2. Targeted Highs/Lows (Weak Points)
*   **IF** a high/low fails to take Inducement or fails to cause a BOS:
    *   **THEN** This point is **WEAK/TARGETED**. 
    *   **ACTION:** Expect price to sweep this level. Do not place stops here.

### 3. The "Single Leg" Trap
*   **IF** price moves in one direction with many small candles but **NEVER** breaks a previous candle's extreme:
    *   **THEN** This entire move is a **Single Leg**. 
    *   **LOGIC:** There are no internal BOS or pullbacks within this leg. It is one impulsive unit of institutional energy.

## Structural Logic Gates for LLM
1. `DETERMINE` if the current extreme point caused a BOS.
2. `LABEL` as "Protected" if successful, "Weak" if failed.
3. `IF` price is approaching a Weak point `THEN` look for sweep entries.
4. `IF` price is approaching a Protected point `THEN` expect a strong reversal or CHoCH.
5. `RESULT:` Accurate assessment of where the "Real" stops are located.
