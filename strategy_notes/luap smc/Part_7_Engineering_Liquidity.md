# Part 7: Engineering Liquidity (ENG LQD) - True SMC (Luap)

## Strategy Overview
Engineering Liquidity is a sophisticated concept where the market creates a "fake" Point of Interest to induce traders to enter early. This liquidity is "engineered" right in front of the real institutional zone (The Extreme).

---

## Visual Setup & Chart Layout
*   **Location:** Always located between the Inducement (IDM) and the Extreme POI.
*   **Appearance:** Often looks like a very clean OrderBlock or a "Support/Resistance" level that retail traders love.
*   **Visual Marker:** Labeled as "Eng LQD" or "Liquidity Pool".

---

## Core Mechanical Rules (IF/THEN Logic)

### 1. Identifying Eng LQD
*   **IF** Price has taken Inducement (IDM) but has not yet reached the Extreme POI:
    *   **THEN** Identify any valid pullback formed between the IDM and the Extreme.
    *   **THEN** This pullback is **Engineering Liquidity**.

### 2. The Sweep Prerequisite
*   **IF** Price is approaching the Extreme POI:
    *   **THEN** It **MUST** sweep the Eng LQD first. 
    *   **LOGIC:** The sweep of Eng LQD provides the final "fuel" for the high-probability move from the Extreme.

### 3. Entry Confirmation
*   **IF** Price sweeps Eng LQD AND taps the Extreme POI:
    *   **THEN** This is the **Highest Probability Setup**.
*   **IF** Price respects the Eng LQD and reverses without hitting the Extreme:
    *   **THEN** This is likely a lower-probability "Decisional" move.

1. `IDENTIFY` the Extreme POI (the very first unmitigated zone).
2. `IDENTIFY` any valid pullbacks between IDM and Extreme.
3. `MARK` these as Eng LQD.
