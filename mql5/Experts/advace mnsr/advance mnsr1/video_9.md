# Advanced MSNR Video 9: Confirmation Entries

This video covers the mechanics of **Confirmation Entries**. These are specific lower timeframe setups used to enter trades either for reversals or continuations, provided they align with higher timeframe key levels and storylines.

## 1. The Confirmation Entry Formula
The formula for a confirmation entry is similar to a storyline but happens on lower timeframes:
- **1H Setup**: `1H Rejects 1H and 1H Breakout`
- **4H Setup**: `4H Rejects 4H and 4H (or 1H) Breakout`

## 2. Higher Timeframe Alignment (Crucial Rule)
A confirmation entry is only valid if the initial rejection (the 1H or 4H rejection) happens **on a Higher Timeframe (HTF) Key Level**. 
- The HTF must be **Daily, Weekly, or Monthly**.
- The HTF key level must be a **Support, Resistance, RBS, SBR, or QM**.
- *Note*: Rejections on a QM are considered slightly less strong than rejections directly on a Support or Resistance.

## 3. Step-by-Step Entry Process
1. **Identify the HTF Level**: Find a valid Daily, Weekly, or Monthly key level.
2. **Wait for Rejection**: Drop to the 1H (or 4H) and wait for price to reject that HTF level.
3. **Wait for Breakout**: After the rejection, the 1H must break structure (break a 1H resistance for buys, or a 1H support for sells).
4. **Locate the New Support/Resistance**: The breakout will leave behind a newly formed, fresh 1H Support (for buys) or 1H Resistance (for sells).
5. **Draw the Box**: Draw a box on this new 1H Support/Resistance from the candle body to the tip of the wick.
6. **Set the Entry**: Place your entry inside this box. 

## 4. Entry Placement: Body vs. Equilibrium
Because the 1H Support/Resistance wick can sometimes be long, you have two options for entry:
- **Body Entry**: Enter right at the body of the support/resistance. This is safer if the market has high momentum and might not retrace deeply.
- **Equilibrium Entry**: Enter at the 50% midpoint (Equilibrium) of the box. This provides a much tighter stop loss (e.g., 5.5 to 7 pips) and massive risk-to-reward. 

*Best Practice*: Split your risk. Place one entry at the body and one at the Equilibrium so you don't miss the trade if price only taps the body and flies.

## 5. Continuation Rule
If you are using this confirmation entry for a **continuation trade** (trading in the middle of a trend rather than at the absolute top/bottom), the setup **must** align with the current HTF Storyline.
