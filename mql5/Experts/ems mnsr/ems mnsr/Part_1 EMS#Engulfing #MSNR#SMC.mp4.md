# Video Analysis: Part 1 EMS#Engulfing #MSNR#SMC.mp4

## Overview
This video outlines the foundational concepts of the EMS (Engulfing MSNR SMC) strategy, demonstrated on synthetic indices (Volatility 10 and 15) using Deriv. The strategy focuses on higher timeframe key levels, structural breaks, and specific engulfing candle setups for entries.

## Core Strategy Logic

### 1. Key Level Identification (HTF Context)
- **A-Shape and V-Shape Levels**: The strategy starts by identifying major support (V-shape) and resistance (A-shape) key levels on a Higher Timeframe (HTF), such as the Daily or H4 chart.
- **Rejection Confirmation**: Wait for the price to tap the key level. A valid rejection occurs when a candle taps the level and its body closes below the resistance (A-shape) or above the support (V-shape).

### 2. Structural Shift (LTF Confirmation)
- Once the HTF rejection is confirmed, step down one timeframe (e.g., if Daily rejection, go to H4 or H1).
- Look for a **Break of Structure (BOS)** or **Change of Character (ChoCh)** in the direction of the expected reversal.
- **Define the Range**: Mark the High and Low of the structural break leg.

### 3. Retracement and the 50% Rule
- Before taking any entry, price *must* retrace at least **50%** into the defined range. 
- You draw a Fibonacci tool from the range high to range low to find the 50% equilibrium level. 
- For a short setup, look for entry zones *above* the 50% line (Premium). For a long setup, look *below* the 50% line (Discount).

### 4. Entry Confluences

#### A. Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
- FVGs can be used as an additional confluence within the 50%+ retracement zone.
- The FVG is identified as the gap between the wick of the 1st candle and the wick of the 3rd candle in a 3-candle sequence.
- **Refinement**: A "perfect" FVG entry occurs when price taps the extreme high/low of an FVG that aligns with structural flow. However, the speaker notes they do not rely solely on FVGs.

#### B. Engulfing Zones (The Core Entry Model)
The primary entry trigger is based on engulfing candles within the retracement zone.
- **Normal Engulfing**: A single candlestick fully engulfing the body of the previous candlestick in the direction of the trend.
- **Engulfing Fail (EG Fail)**: A scenario where an engulfing pattern forms, but the market fails to follow through, and an opposite engulfing pattern immediately forms, breaking the initial zone.
- **Marking the Zone**: The zone is drawn using the body (open to close) of the engulfing candle.
- **Entry Trigger**: Wait for the price to pull back and tap into the marked engulfing zone.
- **Invalidation**: If a candle closes above the marked engulfing zone (for a sell) or below it (for a buy), the setup is invalidated, and it becomes an "Engulfing Fail."

### 5. Liquidity and Targeting
- **Internal Range Liquidity (IRL)**: The minor highs and lows within the current structural range. Price will often seek to sweep IRL before making a major move.
- **External Range Liquidity (ERL)**: The major swing highs and swing lows that define the range.
- **Take Profit (TP)**: Targets are typically placed at External Range Liquidity (the range low for a sell, range high for a buy) or at the next major opposing key level.

### 6. Strategy "Hacks" (Advanced Entry)
- If price retraces to the 50% level but there is no clear engulfing zone or FVG, look for a minor key level (e.g., a small A-shape or V-shape) near the 50% equilibrium. 
- If price taps this minor level, you can drop down to a micro-timeframe (e.g., M15) to spot a fresh Engulfing setup for a sniper entry.
