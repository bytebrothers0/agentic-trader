## Specs + expected results

**PHX1 `MSNR_PHX_Liquidity.mq5`** *(built, v1.00)* — pools + Target Sweeps (Parts 1, 3)
- Pools: (a) wick-fractal swing highs/lows (E3 parity) = BSL/SSL;
  (b) EQH/EQL: >= 2 swing extremes within `InpEQHPips` (2-3) = strong pool;
  (c) session levels: PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL (D1/W1 `iHigh`/`iLow`), Asian-range
  H/L (`InpAsianStart/End`, default 00:00-07:00 server).
- TS per pool, closed bars: wick rejection (`high > pool && close < pool`,
  mirror SSL) OR rapid reversal (marginal close beyond <= `InpOverPips`,
  opposite close-back within 2 bars). Wick-tip price stored (feeds PHX5).
- Rendering: EQ pools thick amber, single-swing thin gray, session dashed
  steel-blue; `TS` label at the sweep bar; pool line formation -> TS (or now).
  Most-recent cap. No rays.
- Expected: double-tops flagged strong, PDH line fresh daily, TS labels only
  on real rejections/reversals - never on sustained breakouts.

**PHX2 `MSNR_PHX_MSS.mq5`** *(built, v1.00)* — TS -> MSS sequence (Parts 2, 4)
- Self-contained sweep detection on fractal swings (PHX1 parity).
- Gate: >= `InpMinBOS` (2-3) continuous same-direction BOS before the TS
  (BOS = body close through fractal swing; Part 2 §1.3).
- MSS trigger = most recent fractal swing low before the TS (bull->bear;
  mirror). MSS = first close beyond trigger. Displacement tag: MSS candle
  body >= `InpDispATR` x ATR(14) = `DISP`, else `weak` (Part 4 §2.1, §4).
- Rendering: trigger-level segment (TS bar -> MSS bar), MSS arrow + label
  with BOS count + DISP/weak in tooltip. Most-recent cap. No rays.
- Expected: MSS flags only after a gated TS; lone BOS never flagged as MSS.

**PHX3 `MSNR_PHX_DojiTrigger.mq5`** *(built, v1.00)* — Doji-at-level trigger (Part 5)
- Doji: body <= 15% of range (Part 5 §2.2), min-range dust filter.
- Context: Doji overlapping a 2-candle S/R or OCL zone (I1/I3 parity,
  `InpZonePips` tol) AND on the correct E5 side (shorts above PD50, longs
  below). Pre-zone-LQ check: nearest E3-style inducement on the approach
  path swept before the Doji -> `LQ OK`, else `no LQ` (Part 5 §3).
- SL ruler (display only, EA enforces): SL = TS wick-tip or Doji extreme +
  buffer; label shows entry->SL pips + `SKIP` if > `InpMaxSLPips` (15).
- Expected: Doji marks only at levels on the right premium side; every mark
  carries its SL budget readout.

**PHX4 `MSNR_PHX_Trendline.mq5`** *(built, v1.00)* — auto TLs + confluence (Part 6)
- Anchors: last two same-side fractal swing points (Part 6 §2.1,
  point-to-point); extra touches counted within tol; max 2-4 TLs.
- Projection to now; `TL x LVL` flag when TL price is within tol of a
  2-candle level/OCL at the current bar (Part 6 §3). TL-sweep tag on
  wick-pierce + close-back (Part 6 §4, E2 shape).
- Expected: clean recent-pair TLs with touch counts; confluence tags only
  where TL truly meets a horizontal level.

**PHX5 `MSNR_PHX_Wick50.mq5`** *(built, v1.00)* — institutional wicks (Part 8)
- Qualifier: single-side wick >= `InpMinWickPips` AND >= `InpWickBodyMult`
  (3x) body, on a TS or BOS bar (Part 8 §2.1 filter kills noise).
- Levels per Part 8 formula: wick-tip line (SL reference) + 50% midpoint
  line. `OCL+50%` tag when mid aligns with an OCL (I3 parity).
- Mitigation: line stops at first close through (E6-fill parity), else now.
- Expected: midlines only on real institutional wicks, never on every doji.

**PHX6 `MSNR_PHX_OrderBlock.mq5`** *(built, v1.00)* — displacement origins (Part 9)
- Displacement: body >= `InpDispATR` x ATR(14) AND breaks structure
  (2-candle close-through, E2 parity). OB = last opposite-color candle
  before it; zone = its high/low (Part 9 §2.1).
- SW tag when the OB extreme swept a pool/level pre-move (PDF "M15 OB with
  strong SW"). Mitigation = full close through far edge (E6 parity); CE
  midline optional on. Most-recent cap. No rays.
- Expected: OB boxes only at displacement origins; SW-tagged ones stand out.
- Note: PDF's "uneven broken-candle zone holds in trend" behaves as a
  proximal OB/FVG zone - no separate indicator needed.
