## FTFL in Continuation
When the market is in a continuation phase (i.e., a storyline is already set, and the market is continuously creating new daily/weekly candles without returning to the origin), the method for finding the FTFL changes.

### Finding FTFL During Continuation
In a continuation, we look at the **Previous Candle (PC)** on the first time frame (e.g., Daily):

1. **Check for Key Level Breaks:** Did the Previous Candle break any key levels?
2. **Scenario A - No Key Level Broken (1-Lap OCL):**
   - If the Previous Candle did *not* break any key levels, the FTFL becomes the **1-Lap OCL (Open Close Line)**.
   - **How to mark the 1-Lap OCL:** Draw a box starting from the body close of the Previous Candle to the tip of its highest/lowest wick. Extend this box one step to the right (into the current candle's space).
3. **Scenario B - Key Level Broken (QM Priority):**
   - If the Previous Candle *did* break a key level, that level turns into a **QM**.
   - In this case, the newly formed QM becomes the FTFL, not the 1-Lap OCL.
   - If multiple QMs are broken, prioritize the **AB QM + FT QM** (First Time QM).

### Refining the Entry (Moving to STFL)
Once the FTFL box (either 1-Lap OCL or QM) is drawn on the first time frame, you **do not** enter the trade immediately.
Instead, you drop down to the **Second Time Frame (STFL)**.
- If your FTFL is on Daily, your STFL is 4-Hour (4H).
- If your FTFL is on Weekly, your STFL is Daily.

On the STFL, you look for your entry point *strictly inside* the FTFL box you drew.
