# Advanced MSNR Video 8: TTFL and Equilibrium

This video covers the **TTFL (Third Time Frame Level)**, which is the final step in refining entries to achieve a small Stop Loss (SL) and a big Take Profit (TP).

## 1. What is TTFL?
- **Purpose**: TTFL is used for refinement. By dropping to the third time frame, you can pinpoint the exact entry, keeping your SL very tight.
- **What to look for**: On the TTFL, you only look for three things:
  1. **Support** (for buys)
  2. **Resistance** (for sells)
  3. **Equilibrium** (when no key levels exist)
- **Note**: The video specifically mentions Support and Resistance, but also includes **QM (Quasimodo)** as a valid level if present. 

## 2. Using Equilibrium
Sometimes, when you drop down to the TTFL (e.g., from a 4H STFL to a 1H TTFL), there is **no key level** (no Support, Resistance, or QM) inside your STFL box.
- **When to use**: Use Equilibrium *only* when there is no key level on the TTFL within the STFL box.
- **How to find Equilibrium**:
  1. Take the box you drew on the **STFL** (Second Time Frame Level).
  2. Find the exact middle (50%) of that box. You can do this by using the "Middle Line" setting on the rectangle tool in TradingView, or by using a Fibonacci retracement tool from the top to the bottom of the box and marking the 0.5 level.
  3. The **Equilibrium** line is your entry point.

## 3. Alternative Storyline: X Rejects X and X Breakout
The instructor introduced a variation of the storyline formula. While the standard is `X Rejects X and Y Breakout` (e.g., Daily reject Daily, 4H breakout), another complete storyline is:
- `X Rejects X and X Break Out`
- **Example**: Daily rejects Daily support, AND then the Daily chart breaks out of a Daily resistance.
- **Trading this**: In this scenario, the time frame that rejected is the same time frame that broke out. You can use an OCL (Open Close Level) formed by the breakout on that same time frame as your FTFL.

## 4. Entry Refinement Workflow Summary
1. **FTFL (First Time Frame Level)**: Mark the zone where the market is coming to (e.g., Daily). This could be an Apex, QM, or OCL. Draw a box.
2. **STFL (Second Time Frame Level)**: Drop down (e.g., to 4H). Look for a *fresh* Support, Resistance, or QM inside the FTFL box. Draw a smaller box around this STFL key level.
3. **TTFL (Third Time Frame Level)**: Drop down again (e.g., to 1H). Look for a Support, Resistance, or QM inside the STFL box. If one exists, set your entry there. If *none* exists, set your entry at the **Equilibrium** (middle) of the STFL box.
